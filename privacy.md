# Practice Tracker Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

Practice Tracker is a practice log for musicians, available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This
policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## The short version

**Practice Tracker collects no data.** The developer has no server, uses no analytics,
advertising or tracking, includes no third-party code that does, and cannot see anything you
record in the app.

## Where your information is kept

Everything you enter (sessions, practice items, studies and projects, goals, tags, journal
entries, recordings and imported files) is stored:

- **On your device**, in the app's own storage.
- **In your iCloud account**, if you're signed in to iCloud, so it appears on your other
  devices:
  - your practice log syncs through your **private CloudKit database**, which only your Apple
    Account can read;
  - audio recordings, backing tracks and score files are kept in the **"Practice Tracker"
    folder in your iCloud Drive**;
  - your History filter (date range and keyword) is kept in your iCloud key-value storage, so
    every device shows the same view.

  Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy. The developer cannot access it.
- **Video recordings stay on the device that recorded them.** They only leave it if you
  export a media bundle and share it yourself, for example by AirDrop or to Files.

## On-device features

- **Weekly summary.** On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the Journal's weekly
  summary is written by Apple's on-device language model. Your journal entries never leave
  the device for this.
- **Tuner and recording.** The microphone is used only while you record a take or the tuner
  is open. The tuner analyzes sound as it comes in and keeps none of it. The camera is used
  only when you record a video take.
- **Link titles.** When you add a web link, the app loads that page directly from your
  device to read its title. Nothing is sent to the developer.
- **Reminders.** The practice reminder is a local notification worked out on the device from
  your own log. Nothing is sent anywhere to schedule it.

## Permissions

The app asks for the microphone (recording and the tuner), the camera (video takes) and
notifications (the optional reminder) only when you first use those features. You can
change any of these at any time in your device's Settings.

## Deleting your data

- Delete individual sessions, items, studies, journal entries or recordings in the app; the
  deletion syncs to your other devices.
- To remove everything, delete the app from each device. Then, to remove the synced copy,
  open **Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage** (on a Mac, **System Settings ›
  [your name] › iCloud › Manage**), choose Practice Tracker and delete its data. Also delete
  the "Practice Tracker" folder in iCloud Drive.

## Children

Practice Tracker collects no information from anyone, children included.
